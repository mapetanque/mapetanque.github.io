The most beautiful courts

Wallonia has 444 pétanque courts listed on Mapetanque.be, spread across its 5 provinces and 157 municipalities. The province of Liège leads with 148 courts, ahead of Hainaut with 106 and Namur with 89. Whether you're from Liège, Hainaut or Namur, you'll find all the Walloon provinces below to narrow down your search.
