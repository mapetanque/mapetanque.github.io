Flanders has 1185 pétanque courts listed on Mapetanque.be, spread across its 5 provinces and 287 municipalities. East Flanders leads with 299 courts, followed by the province of Antwerp with 286, then West Flanders with 222. Whether you're in East Flanders, Antwerp or West Flanders, you'll find all the Flemish provinces below to narrow down your search.
