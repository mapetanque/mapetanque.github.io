Hainaut has 106 pétanque courts listed on Mapetanque.be, spread across 31 municipalities. In Farciennes, the Pétanque Club Le Pays Noir had 7 indoor and 20 outdoor lanes in 2023, 27 in total, which could make it the largest pétanque complex in Belgium.
