The province of Namur has 88 pétanque courts listed on Mapetanque.be, spread across 29 municipalities. The city of Namur has 13 courts, ahead of Eghezée with 6. Champion, one of the villages of the municipality of Namur, founded its pétanque section more than 50 years ago; today its winter championship brings together more than 900 players. In Belgrade, also part of the city of Namur, the José Tyssaen sports centre on rue des Tautîts has 10 floodlit outdoor pétanque lanes.
